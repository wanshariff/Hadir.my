Hadir Design System

Built from the Pairwise spec of 5 Oct 2026, extended for a product that guests aged 20 to 80 open from a WhatsApp forward. Every value on this page is read from src/styles/tokens.css at build time.

Principles

  1. The guest decides in 30 seconds

    Date, place and a one-tap answer come first. Everything else waits below the RSVP.

  2. Trust is visible

    "Pautan web · tiada muat turun" appears on every invite. No pattern here may look like a download or an app install.

  3. Tok can read it

    18px body on guest pages, 48px targets, plain BM. Contrast is tested in code, not eyeballed.

  4. Calm, then celebration

    Aurora sits behind headers only. Tahlil, a memorial gathering, drops the warm accent and the Aurora entirely.

Type

Fraunces 500 for display, tracking −0.01em. Figtree for text, leading 1.55. Roboto Mono for bare figures only: headcounts, prices, phone numbers, URLs. Sentences that contain numbers ("Lagi 68 hari", "11:00 PG – 4:00 PTG") stay in Figtree with tabular numerals. Major third scale (1.25) on a 16px base.

Aurora

Three soft radial gradients drifting over 24 seconds. Only transform animates and there is no filter blur, so it stays cheap on a mid-range Android phone.

Text sits on a panel

Panels are 78–80% opaque with a 12px backdrop blur, so body text never reads against the gradient at full strength.

  • Use behind page headers: invite cover, home hero, host header.
  • Put text on a .panel when it sits over Aurora below a header.
  • Not on /selamat, which wary readers study closely.
  • Not on tahlil invites: data-tone="muted" sets its opacity to 0.
